The bank and online-advisor results in Table~\ref{tab:bank_online_advisors}
use the same observed interval, 204,800--4,999,168 transitions, for
normalized sampled-success AUC; success is measured at its final checkpoint.
The bank uses ten training seeds and each online advisor uses five.
Online advisors have a 120-request cap and retain their original
10M training schedule when reporting the first 5M transitions.
Initial advice coefficients are 0.1 for the bank and 1.0 for online
advisors. These are summaries of the respective study configurations.
