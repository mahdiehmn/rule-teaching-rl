# Saved-artifact teaching diagnostics

Date: 2026-10-02. Status: retrospective descriptive analysis, definitions recorded before computation; not fresh confirmation or a new learning experiment.

Scope: three saved-artifact diagnostics. No training, environment rollouts, LLM calls, bank edits, model selection, or changes to frozen comparisons.

1. Debugging: score every saved KeyCorridor-memory confirmation-panel state and Crafter-v3 development-panel state with the base bank (built without progress facts), the selected progress-fact bank, and the progress-fact bank with only progress conditions/exceptions removed. Keep action preconditions unchanged in that last comparison. Report all panel states, post-progress subset denominators, conflicts, abstentions, coverage, and repeated-subgoal labels. KeyCorridor repetition means pickup with a visible key directly ahead after unlocking; Crafter repetition means place_table after its achievement. These operational diagnostics do not prove that every repeated action is harmful. Select an illustrative changed-label witness deterministically in saved order, preferring a base-bank repeated-subgoal label suppressed by progress clauses. Save predicates, matching rules, source position, bank hashes and the selection rule. The predicate-only counterfactual isolates labels, not learning.
2. Exposure: read all planned cells in fixed suites dk_confirm, mr_confirm, kc_confirm, kc_mem_confirm, dk16, kc_s4 from the existing Vulcan sync. Account for incomplete/failed cells explicitly; validate completed summaries and teacher-free evaluations against frozen cell arguments, matching initial-policy hashes and learning settings for paired contrasts. Report final label counts, labels per environment step and per advisor check, first-label timing, teacher-free curves and learning AUC. Never reconstruct an advice time series from endpoints. Reused source controls remain reused; partial contrasts are marked interim.
3. Quality: score saved MiniGrid bank variants on their task's fixed saved confirmation panel, including unsuccessful and untrained variants. Join learning gains only by the actual bank identity. Do not mix Crafter action-effectiveness with MiniGrid oracle agreement. One plot point is a task/student/bank/weight/cohort comparison, not a seed pretending to be an independent bank. No regression, prediction, new significance claims, or seed-level precision pseudoreplication. Report descriptive paired 95% t intervals; original confirmatory tests are not replaced.

Decision rule: produce plots/tables and candidate interpretation only after internal integrity checks. Missing temporal logs permit endpoint analysis only; missing counterparts permit no paired estimate. Complete but inconsistent records fail admission. An independent reviewer must check the implementation/results before findings are promoted to the paper or shared-facts files. No outcome triggers extra training or paid collection. All diagnostic signs are retained.

This is a bounded follow-up analysis, not a new hypothesis ledger. Outputs: `docs/assets/executable_teaching_2026-10-02/`; code: `scripts/analyze_executable_teaching_20261002.py`.
